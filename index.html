01About

I started in 2007 on databases and enterprise systems: Oracle query tuning, a SQL Server 2000 to 2005 data migration, and the PostgreSQL database behind a national ecosystem-science data portal. Then I spent a decade building data pipelines for genomics research at CSIRO, the University of Sydney, the Westmead Institute and the ANZAC Research Institute, on AWS and on the NCI Gadi supercomputer, and took a Ph.D. along the way.

Since 2026 I have built the data platform behind StockClaw AI, a live market-intelligence product, and Snowflake pipelines for pharmaceutical sales data. The habit I bring from research is to decide how a result will be checked before I produce it.

A pipeline for 31 cancer-risk genes, scored against five Genome in a Bottle reference genomes. The first version failed. A filter change went through written change control, and no panel SNV was missed afterwards (316 of 316), with the shortfalls reported as plainly as the passes.
